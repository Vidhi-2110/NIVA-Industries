LEARN MORE ABOUT OUR ENGINEERING

Engineered from heavy-gauge Galvanized Plain (GP) sheet steel, pre-treated via a 9-tank chemical process, and electrostatically powder coated with epoxy paint. Built for tropical climates and ambient temperatures from -5°C to +50°C.

Fully segregated Medium Voltage (MV/RMU), Transformer, and Low Voltage (LV) compartments with mechanical safety interlocks, internal illumination, and thermal class K10 enclosures tested to IEC 62271-202.
