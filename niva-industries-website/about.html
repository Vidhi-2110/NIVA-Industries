THE NEXT GENERATION OF
STEELMAKING:
BREAKTHROUGHS IN
QUALITY AND SPEED

At Fabrik, we are reaching new heights in steel innovation. Through the acquisition of mini mill leader Big River Steel and the completion of our Fairfield, AL, mini mill in 2021, we now boast industry-best mini mill capabilities to complement our leadership in integrated steelmaking. Our latest breakthrough, verdeX® steel, sets a new standard with up to 90% recycled steel content and a remarkably lower carbon footprint—up to 75% lower than traditional methods.

As we transition to our visionary strategy, our planet becomes our most valued customer. Our commitment lies in providing distinctive, profitable, and sustainable solutions to our clients. Beyond steel, we are dedicated to enhancing the well-being of our employees, our customers, and the communities we serve. The future of steel has never been brighter, and at Fabrik, we eagerly embrace it with a sense of urgency.

GENERATIONS OF
EXCELLENCE

In the past 50 years, Fabrik has transformed from a sourcing company into a contract manufacturing leader. Our success thrives on building trustful relationships with clients and suppliers, achieved through global expansion and service enhancement. Looking ahead, we are dedicated to upholding our tradition of innovation and delivering unparalleled manufacturing experiences for our clients for the next fifty years and beyond.

1980

Fabrik is founded by Joh Colman in Cleveland, Ohio.

2000

After 30 years of growing Fabrik into a leading contract manufacturing company, Joh Colman retires.

2014

In 2014, Fabrik made a strategic move by acquiring Lone Star Technologies, a prominent producer of welded pipes catering to the oil and gas sector.
