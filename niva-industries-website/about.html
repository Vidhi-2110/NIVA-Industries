NIVA Power Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of electrical panels, compact substations, transformer enclosures and precision sheet metal components. With a focus on quality, innovation and customer satisfaction, we deliver reliable and efficient power solutions for industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects across India.

We are your trusted partner in power distribution and automation solutions, delivering quality, innovation and long-term value for a smarter, safer and more efficient tomorrow. Our product range covers complete solutions for power distribution, industrial automation and electrical control applications, all built at our own works in Vadodara, Gujarat.
