Welcome to Niva Industries, Vadodara's leading provider of innovative solutions in the electrical engineering field. Our mission is to empower utilities, infrastructure developers, and industrial facilities through state-of-the-art Compact Secondary Substations (CSS) up to 33 kV and 2.5 MVA. Built strictly in accordance with IEC 62271-202, our type-tested assemblies unite transformers, high-voltage switchgear, and low-voltage distribution in a solitary, secure enclosure.

Our “All Set to Go” engineering philosophy eliminates lengthy on-site civil works. Every substation is fully designed, laser-cut, assembled, and factory pre-commissioned at our Waghodia manufacturing plant before dispatch. Once delivered to your site, only incoming and outgoing feeder terminations are needed. Compared to traditional brick-and-mortar substations, your project experiences a delivery and start-up time reduction of up to 20%.
