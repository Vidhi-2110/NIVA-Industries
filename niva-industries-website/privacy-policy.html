Privacy Policy

How NIVA Industries collects, uses, stores, discloses and protects personal information.

Effective Date: 26 October 2026
Website: https://www.niva-industries.com/

1. Introduction

NIVA Industries (“NIVA Industries”, “we”, “our” or “us”) is an electrical engineering and manufacturing business based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. We provide industrial electrical solutions, including Compact Secondary Substations (CSS), outdoor compact substations, electrical enclosures, fabrication, and related engineering and manufacturing services.

We respect the privacy of individuals who visit our website, submit business enquiries, request quotations, communicate with our team, or otherwise interact with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our website or communicate with us through our website, email, telephone or other business communication channels.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Indian data protection and information technology laws, including applicable provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, and other applicable laws, as and when their relevant provisions become effective.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Personal and Contact Information

When you submit an enquiry, request a quotation or contact our team, we may collect:

  • Name and designation.
  • Company or organisation name.
  • Business email address.
  • Telephone or mobile number.
  • Business address and location.
  • Information you include in your enquiry or correspondence.

2.2 Business and Project Information

To understand your requirements and respond appropriately, we may collect information relating to:

  • Product enquiries and requested electrical solutions.
  • Project specifications, drawings and technical requirements.
  • Installation locations and project timelines.
  • Equipment configurations and capacity requirements.
  • Requests for pricing, quotations, manufacturing, testing, installation, commissioning or maintenance.
  • Purchase orders, contracts and related business correspondence, where applicable.

Please do not submit confidential technical drawings, proprietary information or sensitive personal information through general website forms unless the submission is necessary and the appropriate safeguards have been agreed upon.

2.3 Technical and Website Usage Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the website configuration and technologies in use. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages visited and approximate visit duration.
  • Referring website or page.
  • Website interaction and diagnostic information.

The extent of automatic collection depends on the website's hosting, analytics, security and cookie configuration.

2.4 Information from Business Partners

We may receive business contact information from authorised representatives of your organisation, business partners, suppliers, or publicly available professional sources where permitted by applicable law and relevant to a legitimate business interaction.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  1. To respond to enquiries and requests for information.
  2. To prepare quotations, proposals and technical or commercial offers.
  3. To understand product requirements and project specifications.
  4. To communicate regarding compact substations, electrical equipment, manufacturing capabilities and related services.
  5. To coordinate orders, project execution, deliveries, installation, commissioning and after-sales support, where applicable.
  6. To maintain business records and manage customer, supplier and partner relationships.
  7. To operate, maintain, secure and improve our website.
  8. To diagnose technical issues, detect suspicious activity and prevent misuse.
  9. To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual and recordkeeping obligations.
  10. To send relevant business updates or promotional communications where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.

We will not use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with applicable law or the purposes disclosed to you.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

We will handle personal information in accordance with the legal requirements applicable to the relevant processing activity.

Where consent is required, we will seek valid consent in the manner required by applicable law. Where another lawful basis is available under applicable law, we may rely on that basis as appropriate.

You may withdraw consent for processing based on consent, subject to applicable law. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and certain information may still need to be retained where another lawful basis or legal obligation applies.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information as a commercial data product.

We may disclose relevant information to the following categories of recipients when necessary and permitted by law:

5.1 Service Providers

Website hosting providers, IT support providers, email service providers, website maintenance partners, security providers and analytics providers may process information on our behalf, depending on the services we use.

5.2 Business and Project Partners

Information may be shared with authorised suppliers, contractors, logistics providers, installation partners or other project participants when necessary to respond to an enquiry or perform an agreed business engagement.

5.3 Legal and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, a valid legal process, a competent authority or a legitimate need to protect our legal rights.

5.4 Business Transactions

If our business undergoes a merger, restructuring, acquisition or transfer of relevant assets, information may be disclosed to the extent permitted by applicable law and subject to appropriate safeguards.

We expect third parties handling personal information on our behalf to apply appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers may process or store information outside India. Where this occurs, we will handle such transfers in accordance with applicable Indian law and any relevant restrictions or safeguards.

We do not make representations about the location of data storage by third-party providers unless that information has been verified.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business relationships, fulfilling contractual obligations, resolving disputes and complying with applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information, the nature of the business relationship and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it or otherwise dispose of it securely, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle to protect it against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

Depending on the systems involved, these measures may include access restrictions, secure communication practices, system monitoring, backups and other appropriate technical or organisational safeguards.

However, no website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and the commencement of relevant legal provisions, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including:

  • Requesting information about personal data processed by us.
  • Requesting correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting erasure of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raising a grievance regarding the handling of your information.
  • Exercising any additional rights available under applicable law.

To exercise a right or submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the details provided in Section 12. We may need to verify your identity or authority before processing a request.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims, subject to applicable law.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, business partners or external resources.

We do not control the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites. Visiting an external website is subject to that website's own terms and privacy policy.

We encourage visitors to review the applicable policies before sharing personal information with third parties.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended primarily for businesses, professionals and industrial customers. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information for marketing or business development purposes.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected in circumstances requiring action under applicable children's privacy laws, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with those laws.
