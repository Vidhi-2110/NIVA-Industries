1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how NIVA Industries (“NIVA Industries”, “we”, “our” or “us”) may use cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Our website provides information about industrial electrical solutions, Compact Secondary Substations (CSS), electrical equipment, fabrication and related manufacturing and engineering services.

Cookies and similar technologies may help our website operate correctly, maintain security, understand website usage and improve the experience of business visitors.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device when you visit it. They can help a website remember certain information about your visit or distinguish between website sessions.

Similar technologies may include pixels, tags, local storage, scripts and other mechanisms used to support website functions, security, analytics or marketing.

The technologies used on our website will depend on the website platform, hosting configuration and third-party services that are enabled.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Depending on the website's actual configuration, we may use the following categories.

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support essential website functions, such as security, session management, network operations and form functionality.

Where these technologies are strictly necessary to provide a service requested by the visitor or to operate the website securely, they may be used as permitted by applicable law.

3.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may help remember certain visitor preferences or improve website usability.

For example, they may support preference settings or features provided by the website platform, where those features are enabled.

3.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

If analytics tools are installed, these cookies or similar technologies may help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

The information may include pages visited, approximate visit duration, referral sources, browser information and general website performance data.

We may use this information to identify technical problems, improve navigation and make our product and service information more useful.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

If advertising, campaign measurement or remarketing tools are enabled, these technologies may be used to measure campaign performance or deliver relevant promotional content.

We will configure and use such technologies in accordance with applicable law and any required consent or preference mechanisms.

Important: The categories above describe possible uses. They do not confirm that NIVA Industries currently operates analytics, advertising or marketing cookies.

4. First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set by the website you visit.

Third-party cookies or similar technologies may be set through services integrated into the website, such as analytics tools, embedded content, security services or other external platforms.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms. Their practices and technologies may change independently of NIVA Industries.

We encourage visitors to review the relevant third-party policies when using embedded or externally provided services.

5. Cookie Consent and Preferences

Where required by applicable law, we will seek the visitor's consent before placing or accessing non-essential cookies and similar technologies.

Where a consent mechanism is implemented, visitors should be able to review available cookie categories and make choices through the cookie preference interface.

Strictly necessary technologies may be treated differently where applicable law permits.

Visitors should be able to change or withdraw consent through the website's cookie preference controls where provided. Withdrawal of consent will apply in accordance with the applicable legal requirements and the functionality of the consent system.

A cookie notice should not state that consent has been obtained merely because a visitor continues browsing, unless that approach is legally valid for the relevant technology and circumstances.

6. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most modern browsers allow users to view, restrict, block or delete cookies through their settings.

You can consult your browser's official help resources to learn how to manage cookies on your device.

Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality. For example, some forms, preferences or session-dependent features may not work as expected.

Browser-level settings may not automatically manage all technologies, such as certain scripts or device storage mechanisms.

7. Personal Information and Cookies

Some cookies and similar technologies may collect or be associated with information that identifies or relates to a visitor.

Where this occurs, we will handle that information in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection and information technology laws.

We will not knowingly use cookies to collect personal information for purposes that are unlawful or inconsistent with the disclosures and choices required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

Cookie duration varies depending on the technology.

Session cookies generally expire when the browsing session ends, while persistent cookies may remain on a device for a specified period or until deleted.

Actual retention periods depend on the technologies installed on our website and the settings of the relevant providers. Visitors should consult the cookie preference interface or relevant provider documentation for specific details where available.

9. International Processing

Third-party cookie or analytics providers may process information outside India. Any such processing will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and relevant contractual safeguards.

We do not represent that all information collected through cookies is stored exclusively in India unless that has been verified.
